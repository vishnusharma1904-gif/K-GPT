This policy explains what personal data K‑GPT collects, why, how we use it (including to improve and train our AI models), who we share it with, how we protect it, and the choices and rights you have.

1. Who this applies to

K‑GPT is provided to students and staff of KSRM College of Engineering who sign in with their college account. By creating an account and using K‑GPT, you agree to this policy.

2. Data we collect

CategoryExamples
Account dataName, college email, roll number, role, password (stored only as a secure hash), email‑verification status.
Content you provideYour chat messages and the AI’s replies, files and notes you upload, and (when enabled) voice audio you record.
Usage & technical dataFeature usage, token/usage counts and quotas, subscription and payment status, timestamps, approximate IP/device info, and error logs.
Payment dataProcessed by our payment partner (Razorpay). We receive payment status and identifiers, not your full card/bank details.

3. Data minimisation — we collect only what is useful

We deliberately limit collection to what the service needs. We do not require sensitive personal data, and we exclude and discard data that is unnecessary for running, securing, or improving K‑GPT. Where data is used beyond providing the service (see Section 5), we de‑identify it first.

4. How we use your data

  • To provide K‑GPT — answer your questions, ground replies in college documents, keep your chat history and personal notes memory.
  • To operate accounts, verify your email (OTP), enforce quotas, and process subscriptions.
  • To keep the service safe — detect abuse, prevent prompt‑injection, and review flagged safety events.
  • To support you and fix problems, and to understand aggregate usage so we can improve features.

5. Using data to improve and train our models

We may use content and usage data to evaluate, improve, and train or fine‑tune the AI models that power K‑GPT, so the product gets more accurate and more useful for students. We do this responsibly:

  • De‑identification first. Before any data is used for training, we remove names, roll numbers, email addresses and other identifiers, so training data is not linked back to you.
  • Only useful data. We exclude data that adds no value, as well as anything you mark private and (where required) anything from users under 18.
  • Your choice. You can opt out of having your content used for model training in your account settings, at any time, without losing access to the service.
  • No selling. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third‑party advertising.
By using K‑GPT you consent to this processing as described. You may withdraw consent for training use at any time via settings or by contacting us; withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out.

6. Who we share data with

We share data only with service providers (“processors”) who help us run K‑GPT, under confidentiality and data‑protection terms:

  • AI model providers (e.g. the gateways/models we route to) to generate answers to your queries.
  • Infrastructure & database providers that host the application and store data (which may, at the college’s option, be self‑hosted on institution‑controlled servers).
  • Razorpay for payment processing, and email providers for verification/reset messages.
  • The institution (KSRMCE), in aggregate/administrative form, for usage oversight and account management.

We may also disclose data where required by law.

7. Security

We protect data with access controls, encryption in transit (HTTPS), hashed passwords, tenant isolation so one student’s data is not visible to another, prompt‑injection and leak protections, and safety logging. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to safeguard your information.

8. Data retention

We keep account and content data for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the service, meet legal/accounting obligations, and resolve disputes. You can delete your chats, and you can request account deletion (Section 9), after which we remove or de‑identify your personal data within a reasonable period.

9. Your rights

Subject to the DPDP Act, you may: access the data we hold about you; correct inaccurate data; request deletion; withdraw consent (including for model training); and raise a grievance. To exercise any right, contact our Grievance Officer at [grievance@promptpulse.in]. We will respond within the timelines required by law.

10. Age & students under 18

K‑GPT is intended for college users. If you are under 18, you may use K‑GPT only with the consent of a parent or guardian, as required by the DPDP Act. We do not knowingly use the identifiable personal data of users under 18 for model training, and we do not use it for targeted advertising or behavioural tracking.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product evolves (for example, when voice mode launches). We will post the new version with an updated date and, for material changes, notify you in the app.
