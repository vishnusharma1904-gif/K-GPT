What We Do

We deliver a single, account‑based AI platform that serves an entire campus. One deployment gives every student a private assistant — with their own chat history, personal notes memory, fair‑use quota, and a low‑cost subscription — while the institution keeps central control over content, spending, safety, and data.

The Problem We Solve

  • Cost. ChatGPT Edu and Gemini for Education are sold per‑seat, per‑year, in US dollars — a large, fixed bill whether or not students use them. Individual ChatGPT Plus is ~₹1,950/month, out of reach for most students.
  • Trust & relevance. Generic chatbots answer from the open internet and can “sound plausible” while being wrong; they have no notion of the college’s own syllabi, lab manuals, or question banks.
  • Language. Mainstream tools are English‑first; many students think and learn in Telugu, Hindi and other Indian languages.
  • Academic integrity. Faculty rightly worry that open AI simply does graded work for students.
  • Data control. Student conversations and institutional documents sit on foreign vendor clouds.

How We Do It

  • Verified knowledge base (RAG). Admins upload official college material; every answer is grounded in it first and cited inline as [VERIFIED · filename]. A Strict mode restricts answers to those documents only — for exam‑accurate, source‑true responses.
  • Cheapest‑capable‑first routing. Each query is routed across multiple providers (via our ZenMux + LiteLLM gateway) to the lowest‑cost capable model, with automatic failover — frontier intelligence without vendor lock‑in.
  • Zero‑cost repeats. A semantic answer cache and learned‑answer reuse serve common questions with no additional model spend.
  • Multilingual. Understands and replies in English plus 22 Indian languages, mirroring the student’s language automatically.
  • Document intelligence. OCR converts scanned and handwritten notes into searchable text; figures are extracted into a gallery; maths renders as LaTeX; the model can draw charts; whole chats export to PDF.
  • Integrity & safety. An exam‑integrity mode coaches instead of submitting graded work; input is screened, prompt‑injection is blocked, and flagged events are logged for admin review.
  • Controls & access. Sign‑in is gated to the college domain with mandatory email‑OTP verification; per‑student and per‑cohort quotas cap spend; an admin dashboard manages students and usage.
  • Your data, your servers. The platform can run fully self‑hosted (PostgreSQL, Redis, Qdrant, etc.), giving the college true data residency. On the roadmap: a natural voice mode optimised for Indian languages.

Unique Selling Point & MOAT

USP: The same frontier‑model brain as ChatGPT and Gemini, made ours — grounded in KSRMCE’s verified documents with citations, in the student’s own language, integrity‑safe, and priced at ₹99/month.

Our defensible MOAT (what protects us as we grow):

  • Compounding institutional data. The more the college uploads and the more students use it, the richer and more college‑specific the verified knowledge base and answer memory become — a corpus and trust a newcomer cannot copy.
  • Cost & unit‑economics engineering. Multi‑provider routing, caching and quotas let us sustainably offer frontier‑model help at ₹99 — a price per‑seat USD incumbents cannot match without cannibalising their model.
  • Institutional embedding & switching cost. As the sanctioned campus tool — domain sign‑in, admin controls, the college’s documents and each student’s history, optional on‑premise data — replacing us becomes costly and disruptive.
  • India‑first vernacular edge. Deep Indian‑language support, rupee pricing, local payments and a forthcoming Indian‑language voice mode — territory global products do not optimise for.
  • Vendor‑agnostic architecture. We are tied to no single model provider, so our cost, resilience and margins hold as the AI market shifts.
